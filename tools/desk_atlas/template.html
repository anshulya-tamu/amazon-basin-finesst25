Plate 3

The hydrological year

The river at Tabatinga peaks in May and bottoms out in September. Rain at Manaus is lowest from June to November. In 2023 the low came late and deep: the second-lowest October since 1995 and the third-lowest annual minimum, after 2010 and 2005. The all-time record lows were downstream, at Manaus.

Field notes

One river, several names

Marañón + UcayaliAndean headwaters in Peru. They join near Nauta.
Amazonas (Peru)Past Iquitos to the Colombia–Brazil border.
SolimõesThe Brazilian name from Tabatinga to Manaus. This is your corridor.
Amazonas (Brazil)Below the Negro confluence at Manaus, to the Atlantic near Macapá.

Three kinds of water

White water

Café-com-leite colour from Andean sediment. Solimões, Madeira, Purus, Juruá, Japurá, Içá. Turbid, so sunlight is absorbed near the surface, but fast flow keeps the column mixed.

Black water

Tea-coloured from dissolved organic carbon, acidic, little sediment. Rio Negro. Dark water traps heat in a thin, warm surface layer.

Clear water

Transparent and green, draining the old Brazilian and Guiana shields. Tapajós, Xingu, Trombetas.

The 2023 and 2024 droughts

  • Cause: El Niño plus an unusually warm tropical North Atlantic.
  • Air: anomalies of +1.8, +2.2 and +2.7 °C in Aug, Sep and Oct 2023.
  • Tabatinga: about {{MIN23}} m³/s on 15 Oct 2023, {{PCT23}}% below the median for that date. Low, but 2010 and 2005 were lower here.
  • Manaus: lowest Rio Negro level on record in October 2023.
  • ADCP, 20 Sep 2023: 13,309 m³/s, mean depth 13.8 m, velocity 0.98 m/s.
  • Water: shallow lakes passed 37 °C (one reached 41 °C). The Solimões main stem stayed at 30–32 °C.
  • 2024: the drought returned and broke low-water records again at several gauges.

Who measures what

  • ANA: national water agency. Stage, discharge and ADCP data via HidroWeb.
  • SGB (formerly CPRM): runs much of the gauge network for ANA.
  • INMET: weather stations. Benjamin Constant 82410, Tefé 82317, Manaus 82331.
  • INPA, Manaus: aquatic biology and field temperatures (Val lab).
  • Mamirauá Institute, Tefé: lake and várzea monitoring (Fleischmann).
  • SO-HYBAM: long-term discharge, sediment and chemistry observatory.
  • IPH-UFRGS, Porto Alegre: home of the MGB-IPH model.
